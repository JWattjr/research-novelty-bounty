# Mechanism differentiation

## Nearest comparison

The closest local comparators are ClaimNet Semantic Exposure Graph and Outcome Taxonomy Resolver. ClaimNet classifies semantic relations and exposure among registered claims; Outcome Taxonomy derives a state from fixed categories. This bounty instead runs an ordered sequence of secret-then-revealed research submissions and changes the comparison set when a novel finding is accepted.

## Distinct interaction and state transition

Each researcher commits one digest bound to their sender, campaign, contract, claim, evidence references, and salt. The contract processes the queue in commit order. Each GenLayer assessment snapshots the current registry version. A supported finding judged novel within that exact registry appends a deterministic entry and reserves the predeclared reward for its ordinal. An equivalent finding links to an existing entry without consuming a reward slot. A later researcher is assessed against the expanded registry rather than a stale common snapshot.

The key transition is `registry vN → supported novel entry → registry vN+1 + one reward reservation`; a paraphrase instead links to the prior entry with no duplicate reward.

## Scope of claim

Novelty is limited to this campaign registry. The implementation does not establish scientific priority, global originality, or truth beyond the evidence and consensus judgment recorded here. It makes no claim about ecosystem-wide originality or Portal scoring.
